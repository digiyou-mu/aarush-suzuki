What sets it apart

E20 Compliant

As a part of our effort towards carbon reduction, entire range of Suzuki GIXXER SF/ GIXXER are now E20 compliant. E20 Fuel type is more environment friendly as its use results in lower carbon emission.

Aerodynamic Design

Inspired from the recent European design trend. The aerodynamic design with edgy character lines and lower centre of gravity adds to the styling as well as functional beauty of the motorcycle.

LED Headlamp

Futuristic LED headlamp design, exclusive to the Indian market marks strong and aggressive impression of this motorcycle.

LED Tail Lamp

Gixxer Styled LED tail lamp shares high resemblance to the original tail light design of Gixxer.

Bluetooth Enabled Digital Console

Bluetooth® Enabled Digital console with features like Turn by Turn Navigation, Missed Call alert and Caller ID, Call, SMS & WhatsApp Alert, Over-Speed Warning, ETA Updates, Phone Battery Level Display.

Twin Muffler

The new end cap design for the dual exhaust outlet is exclusive to the new Gixxer giving distinctive look and macho character to the sporty design.

Disc ABS

Single Channel ABS (Anti-Lock Brake System) is the supplemental system which automatically controls the braking force efficiently and avoids wheel lock up due to the slippery road, the sudden change of road conditions…
