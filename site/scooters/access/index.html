What sets it apart

ABS

The New Suzuki Access is now offered with Single Channel Anti-Lock Braking System (ABS) adding rider-assist braking support designed to operate during sudden or emergency braking situations, while retaining Access’s…

Coloured TFT Digital Console

The new Access features a 4.2 inch colored TFT (Thin-Film Transistor) Digital Console.

Greater Torque at Lower RPM

The engine produces greater torque at lower rpm. Maximum torque has also been increased and is now achieved at a lower rpm (5,500->5,000 rpm).

Optimized CVT Settings

The finely tuned CVT transmission delivers satisfying acceleration at low rpm, without having to open the throttle excessively, while its settings are also effective in limiting fuel consumption.

Improved Combustion Efficiency and Engine Performance

This is achieved by implementing changes to the camshaft profile. Specifically, the intake cam lifts higher to increase the amount of air intake, while the exhaust cam timing was adjusted to minimize overlap.

Ideal Combustion Efficiency

Optimized intake port’s shape and combustion chamber shape to maximizes the flow velocity of the air-fuel mixture entering the combustion chamber from intake port.

Fuel Injection System & Optimized Intake Valve Shape

The mounting angle of the injector is also optimized to for excellent combustion efficiency. Precise control over fuel delivery also makes engine starts easier and more reliable, even in cold weather.

Suzuki Eco Performance (SEP) Technology

The Engine is powered by cutting edge Suzuki Eco Performance (SEP) technology which is the forward-looking engine development concept behind the creation of the powerplant for Access.
