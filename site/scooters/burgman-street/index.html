What sets it apart

SEP Engine

SEP Enginge delivers high fuel efficiency and excellent acceleration performance. Its optimized intake system, high compression design, and precise fuel injection enhance power delivery and fuel efficiency.

Greater Torque at Lower RPM

The engine is tuned to deliver higher torque at lower rpm, with peak torque now achieved earlier at 5,250 rpm. This enhances responsiveness during acceleration, especially in city riding conditions.

Optimized CVT Settings

The CVT is optimized to provide satisfying acceleration at low rpm, without having to open the throttle excessively, while its settings are also effective in limiting fuel consumption.

Ideal Combustion Efficiency

The engine is designed to achieve a high compression ratio for strong performance and improved fuel efficiency.

Fuel Injection System & Optimized Intake Valve Shape

The optimized fuel injector angle ensures precise fuel delivery for efficient combustion, smoother performance, and lower emissions. This also enables reliable engine starts, even in cold conditions.

One Way Clutch for Quieter Start

The engine starts up more quietly with roller type one-way clutch. The new clutch springs continue to allow engine starts at temperatures as low as -10°C.

Colour TFT Instrument Cluster

Color TFT Instrument Cluster is available in Burgman Street TFT Edition.

Multi-Function Keyless System

Keyless system lets riders start, open fuel lid, and access storage without using a key. Answer-back flashes indicators to locate the scooter. Loss alert warns if fob disconnects.
