  1. 01

    Suzuki Eco Performance engines

    The Access and Burgman Street use SEP engines tuned for more torque at low rpm, for brisk pickup in traffic.

  2. 02

    E20 compliant

    Suzuki lists its scooter range as E20 compliant, ready for petrol blended with 20% ethanol.

  3. 03

    Connected consoles

    Bluetooth-enabled and colour TFT digital consoles are available on selected Access, Burgman Street and Burgman Street EX editions.
