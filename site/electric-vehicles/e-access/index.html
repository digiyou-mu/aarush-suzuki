What sets it apart

Suzuki E Technology

Suzuki e technology indicates a continued focus on technologies that deliver (A) High Quailty and (B) Superior drivability while benefiting from suzuki`s long history of developing motorcycles and scooters, even as…

4× Longer of LFP Battery Life

Suzuki chose LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) batteries over NMC(Nickel Manganese Cobalt ) for the e-ACCESS’ lithium-ion battery pack specifically because LFP batteries deliver the betterbalance of capacity, durability…

Durable & Safe EV Drivetrain

We believe that truly reliable craftsman ship comes from meticulous attention to details & the legacy built over time. Development test on E-Access follow the same rigorous test as large motorcycles like Hayabusa.

Smooth Pickup with Synchronised Throttle Response

The e-ACCESS improves on areas where users typically find the drivability of electric scooters lacking.

Constant Power Delivery Even at Low Battery Charge

Priority was to provide constant power output, even when the SoC (State of Charge) drops.

Maintenance-Free Drive Belt

One advantage of the belt is that it never requires lubrication, (which is necessary when usinga standard roller chain). The belt employs an auto-tensioner that eliminates the need for regular adjustments.

Suzuki Drive Mode Selector-E (SDMS-e)

Keyless System

This system lets the rider turn on the motor, open the charging inlet lid, or access the underseat utility compartment as long as they are carrying the remote fob on their person.
