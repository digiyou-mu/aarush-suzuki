The Hayabusa is Suzuki’s ultimate sport bike: a 1,340cc liquid-cooled in-line four with the Suzuki Intelligent Ride System, including power modes, a bi-directional quick shifter and traction control.

What sets it apart

Suzuki Intelligent Ride System (S.I.R.S.)

Suzuki Drive Mode Selector Alpha (SDMS-α) groups together five advanced electronic control systems while enabling riders to select individual settings for each.

The Perfect Marriage of Power and Poise

Ultimate performance is born of the perfect marriage between power and poise, and between the rider and the bike. The Hayabusa delivers this in spades. Abundant power and torque offer a more confident ride in any gear and at any speed.
