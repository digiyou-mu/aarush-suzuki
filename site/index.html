    Ask us on WhatsApp
    01Which Suzuki bikes and scooters can I buy at Aarush Suzuki?

    We sell the full Suzuki Motorcycle India range. Motorcycles: Gixxer, Gixxer SF, Gixxer 250, Gixxer SF 250 and V-Strom SX. Scooters: Access, Avenis, Burgman Street and Burgman Street EX. Electric: the e-Access. Ask your nearest showroom about the GSX-8R, V-Strom 800DE and Hayabusa.

    02Where are Aarush Suzuki showrooms in Hyderabad and Telangana?

    We have twelve showrooms, each with sales and service. Eight are in Hyderabad: Attapur, Bahadurpura, Chandrayangutta, Gachibowli, Malkajgiri, Suncity, Best Value Attapur and Best Value Chandrayangutta. Four are elsewhere in Telangana: Sircilla, Jangaon, Nirmal and Adilabad. Addresses and directions

    03What are your showroom timings?

    Our showrooms are open Monday to Sunday, 9:30 AM to 9:30 PM. Call sales on 88852 46633 or service on 88851 06633.

    04How do I book a test ride?

    Choose a vehicle, a showroom and a date on our test ride page, call 88852 46633, or message us on WhatsApp. Our team will call to confirm a time that suits you. Please bring a valid driving licence on the day. Book a test ride

    05How do I book a Suzuki and get the on-road price?

    Visit any Aarush Suzuki showroom or ask us on WhatsApp. The on-road price depends on the model, variant, colour and insurance you choose, so our sales team will give you an exact figure and help you reserve your vehicle.

    06Do you help with finance and insurance?

    Yes. Our team can help with finance, insurance and registration, and will explain every part of the on-road price before you buy.

    07What is the range of the Suzuki e-Access?

    The e-Access has a 3.072 kWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery and a certified range of 95 km as per AIS 040. Suzuki quotes 0 to 100% charging in 2 h 12 min on a fast charger and 6 h 20 min on the portable charger. Explore Suzuki EV

    08How do I book a service for my Suzuki?

    Book an appointment online or call our service line on 88851 06633, bring your vehicle to the branch you chose, and our team will service it and keep you updated. Book a service

    09Are Aarush Suzuki service centres authorised by Suzuki?

    Yes. Our service centres are authorised by Suzuki, and our technicians work to Suzuki's standards using genuine Suzuki parts and oils.

    10What warranty comes with a new Suzuki?

    Every new Suzuki comes with Suzuki Motorcycle India's manufacturer warranty. Cover and duration vary by model and are set out in your owner's manual and warranty booklet. Our team will walk you through the terms for your vehicle at delivery.
