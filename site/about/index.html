A Suzuki dealer that stays close to where you ride.

Our story

Aarush Suzuki sells and services Suzuki motorcycles, scooters and electric vehicles across Hyderabad and Telangana.

Today that means twelve locations: eight across Hyderabad, from Gachibowli to Chandrayangutta, and four in Sircilla, Jangaon, Nirmal and Adilabad. Every location has its own sales floor and service team, so buying a Suzuki and looking after it happen in the same place.

Two of our Hyderabad locations are Suzuki Best Value outlets, part of Suzuki’s programme for pre-owned vehicles. Aarush Suzuki showrooms are listed on Suzuki Motorcycle India’s official dealer locator.

What to expect when you visit

  1. Time on the floorSit on the models you’re considering, compare them side by side and ask as many questions as you like.
  2. A proper test rideRide the vehicle you’re interested in. Book ahead and we’ll have it ready.
  3. Help with the paperworkOur team can help with finance, insurance and registration, and explain the on-road price.
  4. Service where you bought itYour nearest Aarush Suzuki service team looks after your vehicle long after delivery.
