Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Aarush Suzuki (“we”, “us”) handles personal data you share with us through aarushsuzuki.in, by phone, on WhatsApp and at our showrooms. We process personal data in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you enter in our forms: your name, mobile number, the vehicle and showroom you choose, a preferred date, and any message or vehicle registration number you add.
  • Messages and calls when you contact us by WhatsApp or phone.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends to our web host, such as IP address and pages requested, kept in standard server logs.

How our forms work

When you submit a form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message containing your details, addressed to Aarush Suzuki. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Once sent, the message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own terms and privacy policy, and by our team.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, arrange test rides and service appointments, and give you prices and availability.
  • To complete a purchase you choose to make, including finance, insurance and registration paperwork.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with people who need it to serve you, such as Suzuki Motorcycle India, finance and insurance partners you choose to use, and government authorities where the law requires.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If you choose to load a Google Map on our contact page, Google may set its own cookies under its privacy policy. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any purchase or service that follows, and longer only where the law requires us to keep records.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent you have given, or raise a grievance. Contact us using the details below; we will respond within a reasonable time and as the law requires.
