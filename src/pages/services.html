Looking after your Suzuki between visits

General guidance only. Your owner’s manual has the exact intervals and settings for your model.

  • Follow the service scheduleService at the intervals in your owner’s manual and service booklet, by distance or time, whichever comes first.
  • Check tyre pressureCheck when the tyres are cold, using the pressures printed in your manual or on the vehicle label.
  • Watch the brakesIf braking feels soft, noisy or uneven, book an inspection rather than waiting for the next service.
  • Keep the chain cleanOn motorcycles, a clean, lubricated and correctly adjusted drive chain lasts longer and rides smoother.
  • e-Access chargingUse the supplied or a Suzuki-approved charger, and follow the charging guidance in the owner’s manual.
